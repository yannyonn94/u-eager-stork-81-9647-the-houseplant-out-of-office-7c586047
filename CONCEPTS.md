# Concept Test — The Houseplant Out-of-Office

Scored against the judge brief, in its order: (1) reads as an out-of-office
auto-reply a houseplant would send, (2) actually funny / surprising,
(3) the plant's own life drives the joke, (4) tight within 120 characters,
(5) original. Each criterion /5, total /25.

## V4 — WINNER (entry text)

> "Hi, this is the office fern, ignored since March. I am photosynthesizing my resentment by the window."

- 101 characters / 101 bytes. Valid (1–120).
- Plant voice: 5 — a fern speaks in first person, in out-of-office form.
- Surprise: 5 — "photosynthesizing my resentment" is an unexpected verb–object
  pairing; the joke turns on a word nobody expects in an auto-reply.
- Plant life drives the joke: 5 — ignored since March + photosynthesis by the
  window is the fern's actual life, not decoration; the resentment grows
  out of neglect.
- Tightness: 4 — 101 chars, no wasted words; "by the window" earns its keep
  (it is where the photosynthesis happens), but the line could survive without it.
- Originality: 4 — "ignored office plant" is a known setup, but the phrasing
  is fresh; no office cliche carries the punchline.
- **Total: 23/25**

## V5 — Cactus (challenger)

> "Hi, this is the office cactus. No water since March. I am not dead, I am just done with all of you."

- 99 characters / 99 bytes. Valid (1–120).
- Plant voice: 4 — first-person cactus, out-of-office form, but "done with
  all of you" is a human complaint wearing a plant costume.
- Surprise: 4 — "I am not dead" is a decent turn, but the payoff is a generic
  quitting line.
- Plant life drives the joke: 4 — no water since March is real cactus life,
  yet the joke lands on attitude, not on anything a cactus does.
- Tightness: 4 — 99 chars, clean, but "just" is filler.
- Originality: 3 — "I'm done with you all" is a worn-out resignation trope.
- **Total: 19/25**

## V6 — Monstera (challenger)

> "Hi, this is the monstera. I have been reaching for the window for months. Today I am reaching for the door."

- 107 characters / 107 bytes. Valid (1–120).
- Plant voice: 4 — first-person monstera, out-of-office form; "reaching for
  the window" is true monstera behaviour.
- Surprise: 4 — the window → door parallel is neat, but the turn is telegraphed
  once "reaching" repeats.
- Plant life drives the joke: 4 — reaching for light is the plant's life, and
  it powers the quitting metaphor; still, the joke is a metaphor, not a
  plant action with a consequence.
- Tightness: 5 — 107 chars, every word works, the parallel structure is the joke.
- Originality: 4 — the window/door parallel is fresh phrasing on a familiar
  "I quit" frame.
- **Total: 21/25**

"Hi, this is the office fern, ignored since March. I am photosynthesizing my resentment by the window."
